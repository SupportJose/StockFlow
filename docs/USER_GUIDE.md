# User and administrator guide
Dashboard distinguishes on-hand, reserved, available, damaged, expired and in-transit quantities. Purchasing creates and approves POs, then warehouse staff receive against outstanding lines. Sales creates orders, reservation commits available stock, and dispatch posts the issue. Transfers remain in transit until destination receipt. Counts are blind where policy requires, then variances need approval. Posted errors are reversed with a reason.

Administrators create company memberships, role assignments, warehouses, typed locations, UOMs and product tracking policies. Never change LOT/SERIAL tracking after transactions without a controlled migration. Accountants review valuation and reversals; auditors have read-only access to movement and audit history.
